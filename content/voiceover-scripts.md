# Kencho Group — audio guide scripts

Target length: **45–60 seconds** spoken, calm and unhurried.
Record in a quiet room, ~20 cm from the mic, at a walking-pace tempo —
this is a workshop owner speaking, not an advert.

Notation: `//` = short pause (half a breath) · `///` = full pause (one breath)
**Bold** = gentle emphasis, never punchy.

Drop the finished files at:

- `public/audio/guide-ka.mp3`
- `public/audio/guide-ru.mp3`
- `public/audio/guide-en.mp3`

then update `DURATION_SECONDS` and the `GUIDE_TIMESTAMPS` times in
`lib/audio-guide.ts` to match the real recordings.

space. ///
If you're thinking about a project of your own, // write to us on
